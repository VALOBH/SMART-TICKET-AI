✨ About SmartTicket

Built with Flask. Designed for Simplicity.

SmartTicket is an AI-powered support ticket analysis platform that helps teams organize, classify, prioritize, and resolve customer issues faster using intelligent automation, modern analytics, and real-time insights.

Our Mission

To simplify customer support workflows using intelligent AI systems that reduce manual effort, improve response quality, and help teams resolve tickets efficiently.

Our Vision

We aim to build a smarter future where AI assists support teams with actionable insights, predictive analytics, and seamless automation experiences.

Why Flask?

Flask provides a lightweight and flexible backend framework for building scalable AI-powered web applications.

How It Works

STEP 01

Upload Tickets

Upload support ticket datasets or paste customer issues directly into the platform.

STEP 02

AI Processing

AI models classify tickets, analyze sentiment, and detect priority automatically.

STEP 03

Insights & Analytics

Interactive dashboards provide trends, category distributions, and AI-generated insights.

STEP 04

Resolve Faster

Teams use intelligent recommendations and analytics to improve support efficiency.
